Hello! My name is Ptelea and this is my Psych Blog! I am a Forensic Science major at Arizona State University and am currently taking Forensic Psychology there. I originally created this blog for my PSY211 class' final project.

I'm very interested in the role mental illness plays in criminal psychology and I intend to use this blog to explore how mental illness effects criminal behavior and whether or not rehabilitation is possible for those with these mental disorders. I'm here to share my findings with you! Be sure to bookmark this site to keep up to date with my thoughts and findings!
